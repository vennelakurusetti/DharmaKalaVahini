Navarasalu

"Nine emotions, one stage, a million stories."
📍 The Grind Cafe, Madhapur, Hyderabad
📅 12th July, 2026
🕐 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Navarasalu was Dharma Kala Vahini's first-ever open mic and cultural evening — a space where every emotion found a voice. What began as an idea became an evening filled with stories, poetry, music, thoughts, conversations, and most importantly, human connection.

Navarasalu was more than an event. It was a space where people came together to express, listen, reflect, and share the emotions and experiences that make us who we are. From the first conversation to the final moment, every story, every voice, every smile, and every shared thought made this evening truly special.
